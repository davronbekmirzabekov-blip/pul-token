About PUL

PUL is an experimental digital token created on the Ethereum Sepolia test network. The project is focused on learning blockchain technology and exploring future Web3 ideas.

Simple

PUL has a simple fixed supply and a transparent smart contract.

Transparent

The contract is publicly available and can be viewed on the blockchain explorer.

Web3

The project is designed as a starting point for future Web3 experiments.
